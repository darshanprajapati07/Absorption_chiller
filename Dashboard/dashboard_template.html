Validation of the LSTM model

34 held-out runs · 60 s step · compared with the MATLAB dynamic model

The network never saw these 34 runs during training. It is given only their seven inputs and has to predict every output, minute by minute, from a steady start.

1 Summary

ModelMean R²Min R²Mean NRMSE, %

Gradient boosting on current inputs alone has no memory, and the chiller needs 18–47 min to settle after a change, so it does poorly on transients. Adding lagged and filtered inputs helps. The LSTM, which carries its own state, is about five times more accurate again. The ensemble of three seeds is used on this site.

1 How the cycle works

    2 How the twin was built

    1. Steady state in Aspen Plus (ELECNRTL, true species). Generator duty assigned to DESAT, DFLASH made adiabatic; energy residual 0.04 %, COP 0.608.
    2. Sensitivity study SENS-1: 163 physically valid cases.
    3. Dynamic model in MATLAB: 9 ODEs, lumped vessels, ε-NTU exchangers, calibrated to the Aspen base case. 197 transient runs, 291 437 rows at 10 s; energy error ≤ 0.032 %.
    4. Training in PyTorch: 2-layer LSTM, 128 units, full-sequence back-propagation, 600 epochs, 3 seeds.
    5. Weights exported and run step by step in JavaScript on this page; agreement with PyTorch within 0.002 kW.

    7 Limitations

    • The network reproduces the MATLAB model, not a real chiller. Its accuracy on real plant data depends on that model, in particular the assumed thermal masses and heat-transfer coefficients.
    • Runs that crystallised (xstrong > 64 wt%) or froze (Tevap < 0 °C) were removed from training. The page warns before those limits but cannot predict behaviour past them.
    • The equipment in the 3D view is generic and not to scale. Only the arrangement follows the Aspen flowsheet.

    8 Terms

    Weak / strong solution
    LiBr solution before and after water is boiled out of it in the generator (about 57.4 and 59.5 wt% at design).
    COP
    Cooling delivered divided by heat supplied. About 0.6 for a single-effect machine.
    TR
    Ton of refrigeration, 3.517 kW.
    Crystallisation
    LiBr coming out of solution when it is too concentrated or too cold; it blocks the solution circuit.
    High / low side
    Generator and condenser at about 8 kPa; evaporator and absorber at about 0.7 kPa, where water boils near 2 °C.
    LSTM
    A recurrent neural network with an internal memory, suited to systems that respond slowly.
    Ensemble spread
    The difference between the three networks; a rough measure of confidence.
    R², RMSE, NRMSE
    Goodness of fit (1 is perfect), typical error in the output's unit, and that error as a percentage of the output's range.
