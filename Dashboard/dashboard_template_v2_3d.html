Energy check

first law applied to the predicted heat flows

At steady state, heat in (generator + evaporator) equals heat out (condenser + absorber). The Aspen base case balances to within 0.04 %. While conditions change, the gap is heat being stored in, or released from, the metal and the solution. The AI was never given this law, so a small gap at steady state shows it learned the physics.

Words used on this page

Absorption chiller
A chiller driven by heat (here, hot water) instead of an electric compressor. Water is the refrigerant, and a lithium bromide (LiBr) salt solution absorbs it.
Weak / strong solution
Weak solution has more water and less salt (about 57 wt% LiBr). Strong solution has had some water boiled out of it (about 59.5 wt%).
COP
Coefficient of performance: kW of cooling produced per kW of heat supplied. About 0.6 for a single-effect chiller.
TR
Ton of refrigeration, equal to 3.517 kW of cooling. A typical home split AC is 1.5 TR.
Crystallisation
If the solution gets too salty (above about 64 wt%) or too cold, LiBr crystals form and block the pipes. The plant must stay below this limit.
Freezing
Water is the refrigerant, so the evaporator must stay above 0 °C.
High / low side
The generator and condenser run at about 8 kPa. The evaporator and absorber run at about 0.7 kPa, under one hundredth of normal air pressure. At that vacuum, water boils at about 2 °C.
LSTM
Long short-term memory network: a type of AI that remembers the recent past, which suits a plant that responds slowly.
Ensemble
Three LSTMs trained separately. Their average is the prediction, and how much they disagree shows how confident the AI is.
R², RMSE, NRMSE
Accuracy scores. R² = 1 is a perfect match. RMSE is the typical error, in the output's own unit. NRMSE is that error as a percentage of the output's range.

How this twin was built

Aspen Plus

Steady-state flowsheet (ELECNRTL) with the energy balance fixed to within 0.04 %. COP 0.608.

Sensitivity sweep

SENS-1 gave 163 physically valid operating points.

MATLAB dynamics

A 9-equation dynamic model calibrated to Aspen. 197 runs, 291 k rows, energy error at most 0.032 %.

AI training

Three LSTMs learn all 16 outputs from the 7 inputs.

This page

The trained AI runs in your browser and matches the Python version to within 0.002 kW.

Limits of this prototype

The AI reproduces the calibrated MATLAB dynamic model, not measurements from a real chiller. How accurate it would be on a real plant depends on how well that model, with its assumed thermal masses and heat-transfer correlations, represents the plant.

Predictions can only be trusted inside the input ranges above, so the sliders stop at those limits. Simulated runs that crystallised (xstrong above 64 wt%) or froze (evaporator below 0 °C) were removed from the training data. The page warns when the plant approaches those conditions, but the AI cannot predict what happens once it is past them.

The AI predicts 16 outputs directly. Some other values are calculated from those outputs, and are marked ≈:

  • SHX outlet streams 3, A, 5 and 6: SHX effectiveness of 0.61 and 0.65, taken from the training data (±0.9 K).
  • Vapour after DESHEAT3 (stream 7): the temperature drop across DESHEAT3 in the Aspen base case.
  • Flash fractions and recovered heat: the Dühring equilibrium.

The 3D equipment is drawn in code for this page. Each shape is typical of its equipment type but is not to scale for a real 80 kW unit. The layout follows the Aspen flowsheet.
